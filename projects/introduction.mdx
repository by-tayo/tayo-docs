The CTF writeups on this site capture short, focused engagements — a single box, a single kill chain, start to finish in an evening. This section is for the other kind of work: builds that take weeks, get torn down and rebuilt, and are worth documenting precisely because the interesting parts happen between the steps.

Each project follows the same structure — what it is, how it's laid out, how it was built, how it was configured, and what actually happened when it was tested.
