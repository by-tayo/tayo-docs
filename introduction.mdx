## How to Use These Writeups

These pages are designed to be read alongside an active terminal. Spin up the relevant TryHackMe room, then follow each section in order — commands are shown exactly as run so you can reproduce every step.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the Target Room">
    Head to [TryHackMe](https://tryhackme.com) and deploy the target room referenced at the top of the writeup. Connect via OpenVPN or the TryHackMe AttackBox before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the Recon Phase">
    Begin with the reconnaissance section. Run each enumeration command against your deployed instance and compare your output to what's documented — small differences are normal and worth investigating.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work Through Exploitation">
    Move into the exploitation sections once enumeration is complete. Each step explains not just *what* to run, but *why* it works — read the explanations before copying commands.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the Methodology Reference">
    If you get stuck or want to approach a step differently, consult the [Methodology](/reference/methodology) page for a higher-level view of the attack chain and alternative techniques.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The best way to follow along is to have an active TryHackMe subscription and your own deployed instance of the room. Free accounts can still access many rooms — sign up at [tryhackme.com](https://tryhackme.com) and deploy the target before you start reading.
</Tip>
